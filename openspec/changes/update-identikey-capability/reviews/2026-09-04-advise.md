## Blind pass (Why + living spec + cited files, before design.md)

Independent take, then compared. Not a competing design.

1. The hole is real. `identikey-capability-v1.md` already splits
   identity / agency / data. It does not bind a token to a *using*
   key, prove hops, or say why a tokenator for a GitHub PAT is not
   Recrypt D-5's rejected registry.
2. Holder-binding for `redeem` is the right analogue of D-5 §2
   (bearer-plus-holder) when the payload cannot be PRE-geared.
   Verifier-injected `holder(<fp>)` after an Identikey signature
   over a nonce is the only holder class that works for P-256
   enclave keys, which `capability-v1.md` §3.2 says do not sign
   Biscuit blocks.
3. Hop provenance as the Biscuit block chain is the right *format*
   (do not stand up `identikey-log`). v1 nextKey attenuation proves
   monotonicity, not that A, B, or C forwarded. If Why asks “how
   hops are proven,” v1 must say that out loud.
4. Tokenator-as-agency-verifier does not undo D-5 *if and only if*
   the payload is never our ciphertext. D-5 §1 rejected tokenator
   because the registry learns every redemption. That objection
   still applies to PAT copy-out and must be taken, not waved.
5. `put_opaque/4` (`secret_store.ex:148-169`) is unsigned host-local
   0600 blobs, today VM Buzz nsec. A PAT vault on that API is
   host-trust, not cryptographic custody. Protocol must not pretend
   otherwise.
6. Salted Blake3 commitment of the secret, unsalted hash forbidden:
   matches D-5 §3 (low-entropy values on the wire). Fingerprint
   must be auth-challenge §5 (`Blake3(dcbor({alg,key}))`), not
   `IdentiKey.fingerprint/1` (raw Ed25519, SHA-256 stub at
   `crypto.ex:24-29`).
7. Guilds named, not built. Membership lookup on the verify path
   would reintroduce the registry D-5 refused.
8. Holder SHALL must be a redeem *profile*, not global, or
   rbac-design Phase 1 VM-exec biscuits become illegal.
9. Copy-out to Z is the stated invariant (“not down the tree”),
   not “never in the using agent's memory.”
10. Prior send-back (holder global / hop required / Identikey-as-
    nextKey) looked closed in the current SHALLs. Remaining risk
    is fold precision (token identity, replay, hop overclaim),
    not wrong direction.

## Steelman — how the design answers each

- **(1) Hole.** Five questions in `design.md` Problem match Why.
  Decision 1 restates the three-layer table and names `redeem` as
  `right(<secret_id>, "redeem")`. Answered.
- **(2) Holder.** Decision 3 is verifier-side, profile-scoped,
  `{alg, key}` on the wire, fp injected, fail-closed. Spec
  carve-out (`spec.md` “SHALL NOT apply to other agency profiles”)
  keeps Phase 1 VM biscuits legal. P-256 reason is explicit in
  Decision 4 via capability-v1 §3.2. Answered.
- **(3) Hops.** Decision 4: hops optional when issuer bound Z at
  mint; when present, nextKey not Identikey; Identikey hops are
  third-party blocks in `add-capability-hop`. Spec has rejection
  scenarios for identikey-log-only provenance and Identikey-as-
  nextKey. Honest v1 cut. Answered, with the overclaim noted
  below as a fold caution, not a defect.
- **(4) D-5.** Decision 1 takes the registry objection: “A holder
  that already stores the PAT is the honest availability
  dependency.” PRE of the PAT is in Rejected. Spec rejects
  Recrypt ciphertext as tokenator payload. Answered.
- **(5) Vault.** This change does not re-specify `put_opaque`.
  Consumer `add-secret-tokenator` does. Correct split.
- **(6) Hash / fp.** Spec: salted `Blake3(domain || salt || secret)`;
  unsalted forbidden; holder fp “not a SHA-256 stub and not a
  raw-Ed25519 preimage.” Stronger than Fable's nit; the human
  amend landed in the SHALL. Answered.
- **(7) Guilds.** Decision 5 + spec “Guild holder class is not v1.”
  Answered.
- **(8) Profile scope.** Closed in the 2026-08-26 amend (verified
  below). Answered.
- **(9) Copy-out.** Spec scenario: tokenator may return the PAT to
  Z; intermediates who only forwarded do not receive it. Answered.
- **(10) Prior send-backs.** Closed. See next section.

Blind take converged on the decisions the author had already
written. That convergence is the accept signal.

**Use PRE / recrypt the PAT** remains the strongest argument
against Decision 1. It fails: GitHub issued plaintext; there is
no ciphertext-for-owner to transform; forcing PRE invents a
second encryption of someone else's secret and still needs a
place that saw the PAT (the deposit). Hold that.

**Make holder-binding the default for every agency profile**
remains the strongest argument against the redeem-only scope.
It fails for the same reason Grok's finding 1 was correct: a
global SHALL forbids rbac-design Phase 1. Exec already has a
first factor (JWT / localhost; guests get no loopback bypass,
`auth.ex:139-143`). Redeem is copy-out of a long-lived foreign
credential. Adding a holder check later is attenuation-shaped;
removing one is not.

## Send-back closure (2026-08-26)

Verified against current spec text, not the tasks.md checkboxes.

- **Holder SHALL was global.** Closed. Opens “A Biscuit that
  authorizes `redeem` of a foreign secret” and carves out VM
  exec / mailbox / snapshot. A root VM-exec Biscuit without a
  holder check is not a violation.
- **Hop SHALL contradicted Decision 4.** Closed. “v1 redeem SHALL
  succeed with zero hop blocks when the issuer bound the holder
  at mint” plus the issuer-bound scenario.
- **“Forwarding agent's key” was not biscuit-auth attenuation.**
  Closed. v1 hops are nextKey; Identikey attribution is
  third-party, not v1; rejection scenario for Identikey-as-nextKey.
- **Fingerprint encoding.** Closed. `holder(<fingerprint>)` is
  auth-challenge v1 §5. Design: HTTP proof is `{alg, key}`;
  verifier computes fp; do not stuff raw key bytes into Datalog.
- **Human amend (salted Blake3, Gordian deferred).** Closed in
  the current “Secret bytes stay out of the token” SHALL. No
  elided-envelope MAY remains (Fable quoted an older draft).

## Findings

None that block accept. No CRITICAL. No MAJOR after self-audit
and realist check.

Fable nits that remain true and stay **fold/act notes**, not
spec-text send-backs:

1. **“the token identity” is still undefined in the spec SHALL**
   (`spec.md`: “nonce (and the token identity)”). The Named-key
   scenario says “nonce and audience.” Decision 3 names
   `(biscuit hash, nonce, audience)`. Canonical encoding of that
   tuple and which bytes `biscuit_hash` hashes stay `ikp-6yz.2`
   / `add-biscuit-runtime`. At fold, copy Decision 3's tuple into
   the protocol sentence so the SHALL and the scenario match.
   Confidence HIGH. Not send-back: design already pinned it;
   test vectors are out of scope here. Mitigated by: consumer
   tokenator Decision 3 signs `{biscuit_hash, nonce, aud}`.
2. **Nonce single-use lives in design + tokenator, not in this
   delta.** Act copies ADDED requirements only. Without a
   “reused nonce SHALL fail closed” sentence, a second
   identikey-capability implementer could skip the nonce store.
   At fold, take Decision 3 step 1 (single-use until `exp`) into
   the holder requirement, same shape as auth-challenge v1 §7.
   Confidence HIGH. Realist: tokenator spec already SHALLs it;
   this change's first consumer will not ship replayable redeem.
   Mitigated by that consumer spec. Fold, don't re-advise.
3. **“It MAY name a secret identifier”** is RFC-optional next to
   “`redeem` of a named secret” and an inspect scenario that
   requires the id. Read together, the token is bound. A
   skimming implementer could mint an unbound redeem biscuit.
   At fold, prefer SHALL name a secret identifier (or equivalent
   resource fact). Confidence MEDIUM — author can refute via the
   agency requirement's “named secret.” Not scored as MAJOR.

## What's missing (fold cautions, not owed boxes)

- Hop *title* (“provenance is the Biscuit block chain”) must not
  be folded as if v1 identified A → B → C. Decision 4 already
  says nextKey ≠ Identikey. Keep that sentence in the protocol
  file. v1 answers Q4 as “the chain proves nothing was widened.”
- Two fingerprint namespaces will coexist on the Mjolnir host
  until Sites Blake3 cutover. Spec now forbids the stub and the
  raw-Ed25519 preimage. `add-biscuit-runtime` still needs a
  vector that fails `IdentiKey.fingerprint/1` as `holder(<fp>)`.
- `put_opaque` has no Identikey ACL. Protocol-tier redeem does
  not change that. Do not describe the vault as keyed custody.
- Who mints (service Ed25519 authority after owner identity,
  not Identikey-signed authority blocks) is already
  capability-v1 §3.2. Restate one line at fold so no one tries
  to have P-256 sign the authority block for PAT biscuits.
- Commitment `domain` is profile-specified here, pinned as
  `"mjolnir/secret-commit/v1"` in the tokenator. Correct split.

## Ambiguity risks

- `"nonce (and the token identity)"` vs scenario `"nonce and
  audience"` vs design `(biscuit hash, nonce, audience)`.
  Wrong pick: holder proof not bound to this token; replay
  across biscuits. Fold from Decision 3.
- `"MAY name a secret identifier"` as permission-to-include vs
  optionality-of-binding. Wrong pick: unbounded redeem.
  Fold as SHALL.

## Verdict

Accept. Direction stands. Three layers, tokenator-as-verifier,
PAT-not-in-the-token, holder-plus-bearer on the redeem profile
only, nextKey hops for monotonicity, guilds named not built.
Prior send-backs are closed in the SHALLs. Safe to `act` the
fold into `identikey-capability-v1.md` (new sections; keep the
three-layer table; point tokenator at `add-secret-tokenator`)
and the README blurb if it needs the holder/redeem sentence.

No owed boxes. Fold notes 1–3 above are act instructions, not
a second architecture round.

- Biscuit-native sealed / third-party holder vs verifier-injected

- Low-entropy secrets behind a salted commitment (salt+digest on
