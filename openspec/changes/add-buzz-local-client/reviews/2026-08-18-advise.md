## Steelman against

A signed attestation is ceremony inside one BEAM; a process allowlist
would hold I5 today. The spec is buying the vocabulary for a second
trust domain. v1 evaluator may *be* that allowlist.

## Tradeoff

Fail-closed before `pending_messages` drops messages on evaluator
outage. Buzz can re-read the relay. Non-Buzz VM-to-VM senders lose
today's durable queue. Right for I5; implementers should surface the
drop.
