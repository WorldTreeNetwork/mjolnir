  > Thanks for the detailed write-up. Main has moved on since 0.3.10 and already covers the use case a different way, so we're declining both additions.
  > 
  > - **Keyframe on join:** a track sets `track::Info::refresh`, a byte ratio advertised in TRACK_INFO and relayed hop by hop. When a subscriber joins at the live edge and the current group's deltas exceed that ratio, `Demand::wanted` moves past the newest group and the publisher opens a fresh one (moq-video forces a keyframe on it). That replaces the out-of-band request and the `subscriptions_opened` poll. It also works across relays, which a per-process counter can't.
