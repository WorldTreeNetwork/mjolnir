## Verdict

Accept. The amended vault (opaque `put_opaque` + salted Blake3
commitment, envelopes deferred) is internally consistent with the
protocol delta (`update-identikey-capability/specs/.../spec.md:37-46`
carries the same `Blake3(domain || salt || secret)` and the same
§5-fingerprint rule), with the code that exists
(`secret_store.ex:155-168, 498-519`), and with the auth-challenge
standard it defers to. No SHALL in the delta is wrong in direction.
Nothing in the blind take, once compared against `design.md`, would
lead an act node into a secret leak or a second vault. What the take
surfaced and the design does not say is clarity debt for the act
nodes, listed under "Owed" below — none of it changes a requirement.

ADR 0008 may move to Accepted with the pointer from
`docs/architecture.md` (`tasks.md:25-26`). Do not fold the
behavioural SHALLs yet (see "Fold guidance").

## Blind take (verbatim, written before design.md)

1. Pin: value and meta are two `put_opaque` writes under
   `_opaque/secrets/<id>/` (`secret_store.ex:155-168`, key validator
   `:513-519`). Not atomic as a pair. Crash between them leaves a
   value without a commitment. Say meta lands first, or both in one
   key.
2. Refuse: "Blake3 SHALL be real Blake3" has no in-tree source.
   `Sites.Crypto.blake3_hash/1` is SHA-256 (`crypto.ex:24-30`); the
   hex NIF failed to compile (`mix.exs:78-82`). A SHALL with no owner
   is the LEARNINGS 2026-08-16 hazard at fold.
3. Refuse: concept collision. `/api/apps/:app/secrets`
   (`router.ex:58-72`, `lib/mjolnir/deploy/secrets.ex`) and the
   `secrets` body param on spawn (`router.ex:372`) already mean
   "host-escrowed, injected at spawn". The ADR must say the tokenator
   vault never flows through env or `vm.json`.
4. Pin: the fourth Auth branch must sit before `localhost_bypass?`
   (`auth.ex:49-63`) per the moduledoc principle (`auth.ex:20-24`),
   or a Biscuit from loopback is upgraded to `@all_scopes`.
5. Pin: holder fp is `Blake3(dcbor({alg,key}))` (auth-challenge §5).
   `IdentiKey.fingerprint/1` (`identikey.ex:75-77`) is stub-hash of
   raw bytes. Two namespaces.
6. Pin: OTP crash reports are a log channel. "SHALL NOT write secret
   bytes to logs" needs the redeem path to keep `value` out of any
   conn assign or GenServer state a crash report would `inspect`.
7. Pin: nonce must be checked by presence in an issued set, not
   absence from a used set. Restart clears ETS; an absence check
   would accept a forged nonce.
8. Pin: "owner fingerprint in meta". JWT `sub` (`auth.ex:158`) is an
   IdentiKey Connect user id, not a §5 fingerprint; localhost is
   `"localhost"`. Say what owner means in v1.
9. Note: salt + commitment on the token is not confidentiality.
   Intermediates A–C see the token and can dictionary a low-entropy
   "API key or equivalent". PATs are fine. Say it.
10. Tradeoff: opaque loses the owner signature at rest the envelope
    gave; host root can swap value and commitment together.
    Acceptable because the host is the verifier anyway. Name it so
    Gordian's return has a reason.

## Steelman against my take

**Send-back on 3 and 8: an ADR that does not name its nearest
neighbour store, and defines a meta field it cannot populate, is not
finished.** Strongest form: `Mjolnir.Deploy.Secrets` is the existing
"secret escrow" on this host, and a reader of `docs/architecture.md`
will ask why the PAT is not a deploy secret for app Z; and `owner fp`
in meta is exactly the kind of unimplementable SHALL that LEARNINGS
2026-08-16 says must not reach the living spec.

Why it does not flip the verdict: the delta pins the store
(`put_opaque("secrets", secret_id, "value", bytes)`, `spec.md:5-8`)
so no act node can land in Deploy.Secrets; the difference (deploy
secrets follow the spawn and the spawning principal, the tokenator
follows the holder key) is a sentence of rationale, not a decision.
For owner: v1 has one credential system (JWT full scope or loopback,
`auth.ex:46-63`), so "owner-authenticated" has exactly one meaning
today and `add-capability-mint` cannot choose wrongly; whether `owner
fp` is present or absent is a field-optionality note. Neither changes
a SHALL's direction, neither opens a path for secret bytes to reach
a non-holder. The fold discipline for the `owner fp` clause is already
carried by `tasks.md:27-31`.

## One real tradeoff

**Opaque + commitment vs owner-signed envelope.** The 08-26 envelope
design bound the secret to the owner's key at rest and gave
recipients a signature to check, not just a hash. The amend trades
that for something the store can hold today (`put/3` JSON-decodes,
`design.md:31-32`) and a commitment recipients can verify with one
hash. What is lost: nothing on the host attests who deposited; host
root can rewrite value and commitment together and every token minted
afterwards verifies. What is gained: v1 ships without a dCBOR
`verify_envelope/2` extension, and the salted-commitment rule is
stated in one place and inherited by elision when envelopes return
(`spec.md:12-14`). The trade is right because the host is already
the verifier and the Biscuit authority; an owner signature at rest
would only defend against a host that is already trusted with the
plaintext. Say that in Decision 1 so the envelope's return has a
stated reason (owner attestation, not integrity).

## What is solid

- Salted, domain-separated commitment with a 32-byte CSPRNG salt;
  the protocol delta carries the identical formula
  (`update-identikey-capability/specs/.../spec.md:37-46`), so there
  is one commitment scheme, not two.
- Real-Blake3 and §5-fingerprint rules stated as review-rejection
  scenarios (`spec.md:41-52`), true of the design today and safe to
  fold.
- Fourth Auth branch, not `@skip_auth_paths` (`auth.ex:34` is still
  only `/api/health`); guests on `10.200.0.1` cannot take the
  loopback bypass (`auth.ex:139-143`).
- No `tokenator_url`, no new overlay port; the :5432/:7222 INPUT trap
  stays learned (`design.md:97-99`).
- Nonce single-use by reference to a standard that specifies the
  replay store (§7 step 3, §10), not a fresh invention.
- Copy-out as superset, proxy as pattern, both inversions rejected
  (`spec.md:63-83`).
- Decision 2 closed by the human with the sidecar comparison on
  record and a named revisit trigger (`design.md:105-110`).
- Fold discipline written into the change itself (`design.md:191-195`,
  `tasks.md:27-31`).

## Owed to act nodes (notes, not SHALL changes)

- **`add-capability-mint`:** write `meta` before `value`, or require
  both on mint and `value` on redeem (take 1). Define v1 owner: the
  depositing control-plane principal; `owner fp` is the §5
  fingerprint when deposit is IdentiKey-authenticated and MAY be
  absent under JWT/loopback deposit (take 8). Reject deposit when
  `meta` is malformed. Amend the `put_opaque` moduledoc
  (`secret_store.ex:151`, "VM-scoped material") to say host-global
  secrets also live here.
- **`add-tokenator-redeem`:** fourth branch goes before
  `localhost_bypass?` (take 4). Biscuit is the credential at
  `POST /api/secrets/challenge`; nonce store bounded and evicted at
  `exp`. `value` never lands in a conn assign, Logger metadata, or
  GenServer state (take 6); return it from `get_opaque` straight into
  the response body.
- **`add-biscuit-runtime`:** real Blake3 must compile on macOS or
  `mix test` loses its no-infra guarantee (LEARNINGS 2026-08-17:
  recrypt does not compile on macOS; `mix.exs:78-82`: the hex NIF did
  not either). A sidecar over localhost (`design.md:200-203`) is the
  escape if no NIF builds on both.
- **Design/ADR one-liners for Grok, at leisure (no send-back):** name
  `Mjolnir.Deploy.Secrets` as the neighbour and why it is not this
  vault (take 3); a security-considerations line that salt defeats
  lookup tables but not brute force of low-entropy secrets by
  token-holding intermediaries (take 9); the owner-attestation loss
  in Decision 1 (take 10).

## Fold guidance

Per `docs/LEARNINGS.md:10` and `tasks.md:27-31`. Architecture-true
today and safe to fold at accept: the three review-rejection
scenarios under the vault requirement (unsalted hash, SHA-256 stub),
"Proxy-only v1 proposed", "Skip-auth redeem proposed", "Extra overlay
port proposed", and "Blob or PRE object proposed as this vault". Not
true until the matching act lands, hold back: Deposit, Guest cannot
deposit, Copy-out matches the token, Fat agent copy-out, Guest
redeems via api_url, Wrong key, Bytes only, Reused nonce, Failed
redeem. The `meta` field list in the vault requirement folds only
once `add-capability-mint` defines what populates `owner fp`.

Do not implement from this review.
