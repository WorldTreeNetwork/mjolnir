## Independent take and comparison

The [ten-line blind take](2026-11-04-advise-2-blind.md) was persisted before opening the prior review, `design.md`, or `tasks.md`. It used the proposal's Why, the living `secret-tokenator` requirement, and the named Redeem/Nonces implementations and router call sites. Its concerns were unambiguous framing, request and operation binding, nonce burning, retained authorization/commitment checks, and independent negative verification.

The amended contract answers those concerns with eight length-prefixed fields following a NUL-terminated domain, a verifier-computed body digest, exact method/resource strings, and distinct copy-out and HTTP methods. The living holder-required authorization and nonce lifetime/concurrency guarantees remain requirements. The new verification task explicitly targets the boundary that existing tests do not exercise.

The strongest argument against my initial take is that this node need not become a general HTTP signing protocol or an audience-verification service. I accept that narrower scope: caller-controlled inputs used by this handler are bound, audience is explicitly an opaque signed label, and the local single-use nonce remains the replay control. Ignoring legacy aliases is safe under the explicit rule that neither validation nor dispatch reads them. An upstream idempotency header is also a reasonable bounded choice without adding local deduplication. These are deliberate limits, not missing decisions. The later api-proxy migration remains outside this acceptance; old signatures fail rather than receiving a fallback path.

## Prior findings resolved

1. **JSON and body-byte mapping — resolved.** [Design encoding](../design.md#encoding), especially lines 27–40, pins JSON keys, string types, required versus missing/empty behavior, exact text, standard-base64 body transport, and malformed-body rejection. The verifier hashes decoded body bytes and forwards those same bytes, ignoring a supplied digest. The [modified requirement](../specs/secret-tokenator/spec.md#requirement-holder-proof-fail-closed-nonce-single-use), lines 6–8, carries these rules and preserves the empty-body copy-out restriction. The example supplies independent expected frame bytes. Public key and signature remain base64 proof material outside the frame, with the existing key-size and signature verification obligations retained.

2. **Ancillary fields and aliases — resolved.** Design lines 42–46 and delta lines 6–8 agree: audience is not checked against an identity; a nonempty key becomes the exact `Idempotency-Key` header value without local deduplication; an empty key adds no header. `method` and `resource` are authoritative, case is exact, and `url` or other method aliases are ignored without rejecting the request. The egress task and scenario now agree on that observable behavior.

3. **Verification of the boundary — resolved.** [Tasks](../tasks.md), line 6, requires golden bytes independent of `signing_message/3`, tampering of every new signed field and the body, malformed types/base64, endpoint separation, observation of upstream method/URL/body/header, failure burning with no upstream call, old-frame rejection, and retained holder/commitment/expiry/concurrency cases. These are implementation acceptance obligations and remain unchecked. The golden example is an encoding vector (`dG9r` decodes to `tok`), not a valid Biscuit authorization fixture; successful proof tests still require a real holder-bound token.
