## Blind take

Written before opening design.md, tasks.md, the proposed guest-fence spec, or the third review; evidence is proposal Why and api-proxy/src/fence.rs only. No living guest-fence spec is supplied.

1. Pin journal and boot-instance identity to the authenticated host channel; copied guest state cannot establish either.
2. Pin classification to a host-issued identifier whose completeness covers all retained admissions, including missing local rows.
3. Refuse classification by a guest boolean or by plausible-looking receipts alone; unknown, mismatched, or stale coverage must fail closed.
4. Preserve receipt generation as the historical admission generation; ownership transfer cannot rewrite admission history.
5. Require adoption retry to return the same pending or confirmed grant for that instance without electing a replacement owner.
6. Serialize exact mutation validation and durable acceptance with ownership transfer; preserve acceptance when the local write or reply fails.
7. Keep Reconcile faithful to its scope-and-key payload; it carries neither a terminal status nor an asserted receipt generation or digest.
8. Require independent instance authorization and an existing admitted key for reconciliation, including work identified by a revoked owner's grant.
9. Refuse to treat a lease, receipt, or accepted admission as reusable authority to send an upstream effect.
10. Tradeoff: conservative retained uncertainty may reduce automatic recovery, but inventing terminal evidence or fresh authority would break the guest boundary.

## Comparison and steelman

The amended contract answers the blind take and the three follow-ups in `reviews/2026-10-31-advise-3.md`. It preserves the prior adoption and durability corrections while removing obligations that required inputs the guest cannot supply.

The strongest objection from my take is that “exact mutation” validation could still be read as requiring the host to observe the local terminal transition. The explicit Reconcile payload scenario now rules that out: the host authorizes an existing scope/key, while the callback owns the local outcome. Likewise, classification identity is the issued ID, not a hidden version in a copied string. These are compatible boundaries without selecting an implementation.

The historical-generation scenario uses a generation-1 receipt under generation-2 ownership rather than enumerating two receipt rows. The normative rule applies separately to every retained admission, so a history containing both generation-1 and generation-2 receipts has an unambiguous comparison rule. This answers the mixed-generation concern; a second row is useful eventual test coverage, not an unresolved contract decision. Conservative refusal on uncertain history remains an acceptable availability tradeoff.

## Third-review follow-ups

- **Classification identity — resolved.** `specs/guest-fence/spec.md:123` explicitly treats a byte-identical issued ID under the same instance as the same classification. The C0/C1 scenario at line 133 obtains two distinct IDs over the same history, advances C1 through an admission, rejects C0, and accepts the next C1 admission. This matches `Classification.id` and the string-only begin/Admit calls in `api-proxy/src/fence.rs:35`, `:92`, and `:210`; the fixture advances the accepting ID at `api-proxy/tests/fence.rs:245`.
- **Receipt generation — resolved.** `specs/guest-fence/spec.md:123` compares each receipt to its own retained admission, explicitly excluding the current ownership grant. Lines 164–184 cover valid historical evidence, a mismatched admission generation, and legacy generation zero. This agrees with `api-proxy/tests/fence.rs:121` and the separate owner/receipt storage in `api-proxy/src/journal.rs:267` and `:282`. Ownership change does not rewrite historical receipts.
- **Reconcile payload — resolved.** `specs/guest-fence/spec.md:188` and scenarios at lines 204–238 separate Admit digest conflicts, classify receipt mismatches, and scope/key-only Reconcile. Reconcile requires independent authorization and an existing admission, preserves its digest, and supplies no terminal outcome. That is faithful to `Mutation::Reconcile` at `api-proxy/src/fence.rs:67` and its callback at `:270`.
