## Blind take (written before design.md / tasks.md)

1. Pin the rule the living spec already states in its own preamble:
   a SHALL exists here only when an implementing landing made it true.
   `add-honor-being` is the architecture write. Its ADDED block is an
   ADR argument in delta clothing, not a list of built things.
2. Refuse a mechanical "merge remaining ADDED SHALLs". Three of the five
   ADDED requirements are wholly or partly unbuilt; promoting them writes
   a living spec that lies about the system.
3. Pin: **A3 consent is not built.** Nothing in this repo gates device
   creation on a fresh WebAuthn assertion or appends
   `action = add_device`. `GitSigning.mint/1` generates a keypair and
   writes two opaque blobs; `xid` and `credential_id` are *optional*
   metadata nobody sets at mint. The scenario "no implicit device
   without A3 consent" would be a false SHALL today.
4. Pin: the `credentials` row of kind `ssh_git` is created **out of this
   repo** (identikey-core). Mjolnir only stores the public half and calls
   `revoke_device` on teardown. A honor-being SHALL asserting the row's
   existence is unverifiable from here; it belongs to the identikey
   capability or stays in ADR 0011.
5. Pin: **Forgejo registration is not built.** `git_signing_forgejo_revoke`
   is the only hook and it defaults to `:not_wired` — a revoke with no
   matching register. The delta's clause "SHALL be registered for push on
   the Forgejo remote …" must be stripped, not merged into the already-living
   git-signing requirement. It is `add-honor-git-remote`.
6. Pin: **CORS is not built** (`update-hypersigil-store-cors`). Do not fold
   "Prod Medusa CORS SHALL list that exact ticket origin".
7. Pin: the passkey requirement **landed** (`add-identikey-being-client`,
   2026-09-20) but its SHALLs live in `web-pty-edge`. Copying it into
   honor-being puts one truth in two capabilities and guarantees drift.
   Cross-reference; do not duplicate.
8. Pin: "Dev preview on the ticket URL, prod API" and the git-signing
   inject are **already living** (seeded 2026-09-19, folded 2026-09-20).
   Re-adding them is the duplication the packet warns about for
   `git_signing_key` — the same hazard applies to the Vite/ticket
   requirement, which the packet does not name.
9. Tradeoff: strict discipline leaves `honor-being` thin — the being-as-device
   shape survives only in ADR 0011 and the `docs/architecture.md` paragraph.
   That is the right trade. The living spec is "what is built"; the ADR is
   where not-yet-built shape is allowed to be whole. A thin spec is legible;
   an aspirational one is a trap for the next reader.
10. Therefore the honest merge for this fold is **close to empty**: archive
    the change, extend the living preamble's provenance so it records that
    the architecture change folded here, and carry nothing new. If anything
    at all is promoted, it is at most the landed half of Requirement 1
    (`owner_id` equals the friend's XID as the public OIDC `sub`) — and even
    that is arguably `web-pty-edge`'s, per `docs/architecture.md:432-437`.

## The one thing the folder must get right

`goal` in the packet says "Merge remaining ADDED SHALLs". Read
literally against the delta, that promotes A3 consent, Forgejo
registration, and CORS into living truth. **Do not.** Resolve "remaining"
as *remaining and landed and not already living elsewhere* — which, after
`add-identikey-being-client` and `add-vm-git-subkey` took their halves,
is the empty set plus provenance. The packet's own `out_of_scope` already
fences Forgejo registration; the same logic fences A3 and CORS, which it
does not name only because it did not enumerate them.

Steelman for merging more: a reader landing on `honor-being` gets grok +
Vite + a git key and no statement of *whose* machine it is — the shape the
change exists to fix. Answer: that statement is in `docs/architecture.md`
(415-445) and ADR 0011, both already pointing here, and the living
preamble already names the deferred pieces and their owning changes. The
gap is navigational, not substantive, and one provenance sentence closes
it without minting a false SHALL.
