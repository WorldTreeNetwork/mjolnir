## Blind take

I wrote this before opening `tasks.md` or `reviews/2026-10-20-advise-4.md`.

1. **Pin:** instance isolation by exact slice membership, not by name prefix. Today's code already shows how a prefix leaks. `find_orphan_hypervisors/1` matches with `String.contains?(line, socket_dir)`, and production's `/tmp/mjolnir` is a substring of test's `/tmp/mjolnir-test`. That means a production sweep kills test hypervisors today. The tap scoping (`mj-`/`mjt-`, mjolnir-0ut) is the pattern that works.
2. **Pin:** systemd slice names nest on `-`. `mjolnir-vms.slice` is a child of `mjolnir.slice`, and a test slice named `mjolnir-vms-test.slice` would be a child of production's slice. "Its own slice" has to mean the exact `Slice=`, not the cgroup subtree.
3. **Pin:** `clean_stale_sockets/1` currently unlinks every `.sock` and `_vsock` in the directory. Attach needs the CH API socket and the vsock UDS to still be bound. The contract has to name them.
4. **Pin:** `terminate/2` calls `cleanup/2` whenever `hypervisor_port` is set, including on `:shutdown`. Detach has to skip that whole call, not just close the port.
5. **Pin:** `Health.Monitor` runs `Reconcile.run/0` on every tick. `do_run/0` treats "not registered" as "stranded", and `execute/1` calls `VM.resume/1`, which boots. So the attach rule has to hold on a tick as well as at startup. Otherwise a live scope gets a second VMM on the same virtiofs rootfs.
6. **Pin:** a `failed` scope stays loaded and holds its name. Count it as a death and clear the name before the cold resume.
7. **Refuse:** a watcher that restarts guests, and any kill keyed on a `ps` command line.
8. **Refuse:** cold resume while the unit is active.
9. **Tradeoff:** every decision row comes down to systemd's current state plus StateStore intent. The watcher unit only adds history (Result) and the live death signal, and a D-Bus subscription inside the BEAM could also supply that live signal. So the second unit is paid for by keeping deaths that happen during an outage. It is never needed for correctness. That is a fair price, but uepi.3 should keep the watcher dumb.
10. **Note:** "the scope ends when cloud-hypervisor exits" depends on virtiofsd exiting when its vhost-user frontend disconnects, or on a wrapper that stops the scope. It is a contract line that needs its own test, because systemd does not provide it.

The scenario "Another instance does not reap this slice" has a second BEAM with a different namespace and a readable StateStore, and the guest's scope is not stopped. The watcher requirement and the true-orphan scenario both say "this instance's slice". ADR Decision ¶1 says the same, word for word. |
| **C2:** `failed` is a death, and the loaded name is cleared before cold resume | **Closed.** Spec Liveness: "ActiveState `failed` is a death the same way inactive is. Before a cold resume the BEAM SHALL clear a loaded non-active unit of that name (`reset-failed` or `CollectMode=inactive-or-failed`)…". The scenario "An OOM-killed scope is cold-resumed" asserts that the name is cleared and that `resume_failures` does not grow. `failed` is also in the missing-unit requirement, in the fail-safe requirement ("gone, inactive, or `failed` is a death … including when this file cannot be read"), in the restarter requirement, in the cold-resume scenario, and in the ADR liveness paragraph and death row. |

Mapped against my take: item 3 is closed by N1. Item 4 is covered by "Orchestrator shutdown SHALL detach and SHALL NOT signal the scope". Item 5 is covered by "Reconcile, from startup or from a Health.Monitor tick, SHALL attach". Item 6 is C2. Items 7 and 8 are the restarter and active-scope requirements. Item 1 is C1. It also fixes the `socket_dir` substring leak, because "`Cleanup.sweep` SHALL NOT kill a process because its command line contains the socket directory" removes the only consumer of that match.

I checked the rows against each other again after C1 and C2 went in. The death rows (watcher saw death / unit gone, inactive, or `failed` / readable record with no entry and unit not active) and the attach rows (active and `:running`, with or without a readable record) are disjoint on systemd's ActiveState. Stopped, dormant, failed-intent, and orphan are disjoint on intent. Anything else ends in the existing retirement limit. No case has two outcomes, and no case is missing one.

## Steelman against my take

Item 2 is the only part of my take the contract does not state literally. The contract says "derived from config the same way `tap_prefix` is" and "only scopes in its own slice". Read honestly, "in its own slice" already means membership in that unit, and a nested child slice is a different unit. The existing pattern (`mj-`/`mjt-`) does not overlap. An implementer who names the test slice `mjolnir-vms-test.slice` and enumerates the cgroup subtree fails the scenario "Another instance does not reap this slice" when it runs in the reverse direction, because the requirement sentence binds both directions. So this is implementation guidance for `mjolnir-uepi.3`, not a gap in the contract. Item 9 is a design preference that the steer already settled. Item 10 is a test obligation on uepi.3, and it is already a SHALL ("The scope SHALL end when cloud-hypervisor exits").

## Non-blocking (for `mjolnir-uepi.3`)

- **N1:** choose instance names so that neither slice nests under the other in systemd's dash hierarchy, and so that neither scope prefix is a string prefix of the other. Classify by the unit's exact `Slice=` property, not by a cgroup subtree walk or a name `starts_with?`. Today's `/tmp/mjolnir` ⊂ `/tmp/mjolnir-test` substring kill is the bug to avoid repeating. Test the scenario in both directions.
- **N2:** "The scope SHALL end when cloud-hypervisor exits" needs a test where cloud-hypervisor is killed and virtiofsd is left running. If virtiofsd outlives its frontend, the scope stays active and the guest is attached when it should be cold-resumed.
- **N3:** ADR ¶ "On startup" says the BEAM "reads the watcher" first. The spec says it subscribes and then reads. The spec is the stronger rule and governs. No edit is needed.

## What I accept

I accept the contract `guest-lifetime` (`openspec/changes/add-guest-reattach/specs/guest-lifetime/spec.md`) plus the ADR 0014 Decision as revised on 2026-10-20, both as they stand in the working tree:

- Each guest's cloud-hypervisor and all of its virtiofsd run in one systemd scope, in a per-instance slice outside `mjolnir.service`.
- A watcher outside the service only witnesses. Its record survives a reboot.
- systemd's current state decides liveness, and `failed` counts as dead with its name reclaimed.
- The BEAM is the only restarter. It attaches any active `:running` scope, whoever finds it. It sends any unit that is gone, inactive, or failed through Reconcile's plan. It finishes an interrupted stop or dormancy, never boots a `:failed` guest, and touches nothing when the StateStore is unreadable.

This accept covers the decision and the contract only. `mjolnir-uepi.3` and `mjolnir-uepi.4` stay open.
