## Steelman and implementation boundary

My strongest alternative concern was tying policy to the snapshot for simpler
consistency. The author's explicit site-wide choice answers it: HEAD rollback
does not roll back policy, and recovery has an authoritative record to copy.
Changing HEAD/manifest signing schemas would add compatibility cost without
being necessary for that ownership model. Likewise, a bounded stale park
directory is an acceptable freshness tradeoff for local miss serving.

The atomic-window and failed-publish clauses are obligations, not evidence of
an implemented transaction. Two independent file renames alone do not prove
them. Act must verify concurrent publication, failed publication, policy reset,
rollback/rematerialization, and the snapshot/policy selection used by an open
connection. SecretStore currently recognizes HEAD, manifest, and alias signing
types (`lib/mjolnir/secret_store.ex:693`); the new policy type needs its own
verified path. These are implementation risks covered by the existing R1 box,
not grounds to demand product fixes before accepting this contract.
