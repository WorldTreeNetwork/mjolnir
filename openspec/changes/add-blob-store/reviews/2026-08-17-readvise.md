## Tradeoff

**Independent boring durability vs native mesh transfer.** B2-direct
buys “destroy the door, hash still GETs.” iroh-blobs buys verified
E2E transfer on a fabric that already speaks Iroh — and no
independent copy unless it drains. They are sequenced. MinIO does
neither job that split does not already cover.

A smaller tension: **our door vs S3-as-client-contract.** The door
is another process. It is also the only place hash-refuse and
“callers never hold B2 keys” can live. Cost is `add-blob-api` is a
service, not a config line.

## Findings (nits, not blockers)

- Door-not-S3 is design-only. SHALL it on `add-blob-api` so fold
  does not lose Decision 1b.
- `spec.md` destroy scenario says “B2 or a replacement provider.”
  Tighten to B2 as the durability proof.
- Recrypt `put_with_outboard` does not hash-check
  (`recrypt-storage/src/s3.rs:217–248`). Wrap it; exists-then-skip
  so versioning-on does not create a second object on re-PUT.
- Door face is a 3-way OR. Pin HTTP to our door first. Do not stuff
  GiB through `deliver_message` (`MAX_PAYLOAD` 16 MB,
  `mjolnir_protocol/src/lib.rs:48`).
- Prefer Sites’ existing `PUT /storage/blob/b3/{hash}` shape unless
  `add-blob-api` records why not.
- Prefer a door *process* on an existing VM, not a new store guest.
- Do not hash through `Mjolnir.Sites.Crypto.blake3_hash/1` (SHA-256
  stub).
- MinIO may still appear later as *a* cache. Not a landing.
